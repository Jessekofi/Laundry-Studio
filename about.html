About Laundry Studio

Your trusted laundry partner in Mount Ayliff

Our History

Laundry Studio was founded in December 2023 by a young entrepreneur named Jesse Yeboah in Mount Ayliff, Eastern Cape. Jesse noticed that local residents lacked access to professional, reliable laundry services and decided to fill this gap in the market.

Starting with basic washing and ironing services, Laundry Studio quickly built a reputation for quality and dependability. The business has since expanded to include specialised shoe cleaning, garment dyeing, and comprehensive laundry services.

Today, Laundry Studio serves a growing customer base of busy professionals, families, students, and elderly residents who appreciate the convenience and professionalism the business provides.

Our Mission

"Providing dependable, clean, fresh-scented, and efficient laundry services that free up our customers' time."

Our Vision

"To grow the business to a scale where multiple branches can be opened in nearby towns, making professional laundry services accessible to more communities."

Our Commitment to Quality

At Laundry Studio, we understand that your clothes are important to you. That's why we handle every item with care and attention to detail.
